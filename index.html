PACT Atelier

Learn what matters in real work

PACT stands for Practical Applied Concepts and Training. An atelier was a craftsman's workshop. Apprentices learned the trade there by working on real commissions, mistakes included, until the skill was theirs.

We build courses the same way. Real material, realistic conditions, and the failures left in, because that is where most of the learning happens.
