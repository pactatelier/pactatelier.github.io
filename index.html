How an atelier works
1

Real material

An atelier was a craftsman's workshop. Apprentices learned on live commissions, not exercises. Our courses use production-scale projects with real history and real constraints.

2

Failures left in

Things go wrong in real work, so they go wrong on camera too. You watch problems get diagnosed and fixed instead of edited out, and you learn to recover rather than to follow along.

3

Theory on demand

Concepts show up when a task needs them, at the depth the task needs. Nothing is taught in the abstract just to make a course longer.

About

PACT stands for Practical Applied Concepts and Training.

We are a small studio making courses for people who want skills they can use at work, in the job they already have. The test we hold every lecture to is simple: does this survive contact with a real task on a normal day.

The method comes from experience, not theory. Our courses are built by engineers who have spent years shipping and maintaining software that runs on millions of websites, where code you didn't write is the everyday reality and mistakes have consequences.
